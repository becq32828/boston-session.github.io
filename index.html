Start here

Three things to do before the first demo. It takes about a minute.

1

Open Copilot

Go to m365copilot.com, or open the Copilot app or Copilot in Teams. Sign in with your Dover work account.

2

Look for the shield

A shield icon near the chat box means enterprise data protection is on. Your prompts stay inside Dover's Microsoft 365 boundary.

3

Have a file ready

Pick something of your own that isn't sensitive: a supplier quote, two spec sheets, a notice, or a spreadsheet of monthly numbers.

1. Copy→ 2. Paste into Copilot→ 3. Change the [orange words]→ 4. Press Enter→ 5. Check the answer

Which Copilot do you have?

Not sure? If Copilot Chat shows Work and Web options at the top, you have the licence. Prompts 1–4 work for everyone; 5–8 need the licence.

Using Copilot safely at Dover

Copilot works inside the controls Dover already has. Your permissions, sensitivity labels and data loss prevention policies all still apply.

Do

  • Use your own work files and examples
  • Remove names and details you don't need
  • Check every fact and number before sharing
  • Treat every answer as a first draft

Don't

  • Paste confidential or restricted data
  • Upload personal, HR, medical, legal or pay information
  • Use Copilot to make decisions about people
  • Send output without reading it first

Turned off at Dover today

  • AI image, video and audio generation
  • Full meeting recordings and transcripts
  • Copilot access to SharePoint sites and shared channels
  • Custom agents and extensions

Part 1: Copilot Chat

Everyone · included with Microsoft 365

These four work for every Dover employee. Copilot Chat uses the web and the files you give it.

1

Upload a file

Summarise a file and find what to negotiate

Copilot Chat · everyone
This one needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. Watch the demo, or switch the toggle at the top if you have one.

Where

Copilot Chat. Select + (or the paperclip) → Upload, and pick your file.

You'll need

One of your own documents. Example: a supplier quote, a contract summary, a proposal.

Prompt

Summarise this [supplier quote] in five bullets. Then list the five terms I should negotiate, with one line on why each one matters to us.

What you should see

Five short bullets, then a numbered list of terms (price, payment terms, lead time, warranty and so on), each with a reason.

Then try

If it doesn't work Nothing happens after upload? Wait for the file name to appear in the chat box before you press Enter.

2

Rewrite

Rewrite for the shop floor, in two languages

Copilot Chat · everyone
This one needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. Watch the demo, or switch the toggle at the top if you have one.

Where

Copilot Chat. Upload the document, or paste the text straight into the chat box.

You'll need

A notice, bulletin, procedure or announcement your teams need to read.

Prompt

Rewrite this [safety bulletin] for shop-floor teams. Use plain English, short sentences and no jargon. Keep every instruction and number exactly as written. Then give me the same text in [Spanish].

What you should see

A shorter, plainer version in English, followed by the translation. Every number and instruction should still be there.

Then try

If it doesn't work Always have a fluent speaker check the translation before it goes on a wall.

3

Compare

Compare two documents side by side

Copilot Chat · everyone
This one needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. Watch the demo, or switch the toggle at the top if you have one.

Where

Copilot Chat. Upload both files into the same message.

You'll need

Two similar documents. Example: two spec sheets, two quotes, two versions of a procedure.

Prompt

Compare these two [pump spec sheets] in a table with a row for each of [flow, pressure, materials, price]. Add a final row with the key differences, and flag anything one document states that the other leaves out.

What you should see

A table with one column per document, plus a row of differences and gaps.

Then try

If it doesn't work If it only reads one file, upload them again together in a single message.

4

Prompt coach

Let Copilot coach your prompt

Copilot Chat · everyone
This one needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. Watch the demo, or switch the toggle at the top if you have one.

Where

Copilot Chat. No file needed.

You'll need

A task you do often and would like help with.

Prompt

I want Copilot to help me with [describe your task in one sentence]. Before you answer, ask me three questions that would make this request better.

What you should see

Three questions about your audience, format or source. Answer them in your next message.

Then try

If it doesn't work Short on ideas? Try: “writing the weekly IT status update for my OpCo”.

Part 2: Microsoft 365 Copilot

Licensed users

These use your own email, meetings, chats and files. They're all included in the licence; there's no extra usage charge.

5

Scheduled prompt

Get a briefing every Monday, automatically

Microsoft 365 Copilot · licensed
This one needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. Watch the demo, or switch the toggle at the top if you have one.

Where

Copilot Chat, Work mode. Run the prompt once. Then point at your prompt, select … More → Schedule this prompt, and choose Weekly, Monday, 7:00 AM.

You'll need

Nothing to upload. Copilot uses your own email, meetings and chats.

Prompt

Brief me on last week's emails, meetings and chats about [project or customer name]. Give me: what changed, decisions made, open actions with owners, and anything waiting on me. Keep it under one page.

What you should see

A one-page brief. Once it's scheduled, a fresh one arrives every Monday morning.

Then try

If it doesn't work No Schedule option? It is not switched on for your account yet. Ask your OpCo IT lead.

6

Workspace

Work across a set of files with a Workspace

Microsoft 365 Copilot · licensed
This one needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. Watch the demo, or switch the toggle at the top if you have one.

Where

Copilot app → Workspaces (previously called Notebooks) → New. Add three to five of your own files from OneDrive.

You'll need

A handful of related files: plans, reports, forecasts, meeting notes.

Prompt

Using the files in this workspace, draft a one-page summary of [next quarter's demand risks]. Group the points by theme, say which file each point comes from, and end with three questions I should raise at [our S&OP meeting].

What you should see

A one-page summary with each point traced to the file it came from.

Then try

If it doesn't work Use OneDrive files. SharePoint sites are not available to Copilot at Dover today.

7

Agent Mode in Excel

Build a variance view in Excel

Microsoft 365 Copilot · licensed
This one needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. Watch the demo, or switch the toggle at the top if you have one.

Where

Open your spreadsheet in Excel. Click in your data and press Ctrl+T to make it a table. Then open Copilot and switch on Agent Mode.

You'll need

A spreadsheet with monthly numbers, such as actual and budget by cost centre.

Prompt

Build a monthly variance view from this sheet comparing [actual] to [budget] by [cost centre]. Add a variance % column, flag anything over 10%, and add a chart of the five biggest variances. Explain each step you take.

What you should see

Copilot works step by step: it adds a column, applies the highlighting, then builds a chart.

Then try

If it doesn't work No Agent Mode? Use regular Copilot in Excel and ask for one step at a time.

8

Teams + Outlook

Recap the meeting, then send the follow-up

Microsoft 365 Copilot · licensed
This one needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence. Watch the demo, or switch the toggle at the top if you have one.

Where

Step A, in Teams, while the meeting is still running: select Copilot in the meeting controls and run the first prompt. Copy the answer.
Step B, in Outlook: start a new email, select Copilot → Help me write, and run the second prompt with the recap pasted in.

You'll need

A Teams meeting you are in, ideally one that is wrapping up.

Step A prompt, in Teams

Recap this meeting so far: decisions made, action items with owner and due date, and open questions. Format it as a table.

Step B prompt, in Outlook

Draft a follow-up email to the meeting attendees using the recap below. Thank them, list the decisions and the actions with owners and dates, and ask for corrections by [Friday]. Keep it under 200 words.

[paste the recap here]

What you should see

A recap table from Teams, then a ready-to-edit email draft in Outlook.

Then try

If it doesn't work Important at Dover: Copilot can only recap a meeting while it is still running. There is no transcript afterwards. Run Step A before the meeting ends. If you missed it, paste your own typed notes into Step B instead.

Keep going

More prompts to try this week, grouped by app.

Brief from a document

Using the document open here, draft a one-page briefing for [OpCo site leads]: purpose, what changes, who it affects, what they need to do, and by when.

FAQ for your teams

Turn this document into a ten-question FAQ in plain English for [end users]. Keep every date and requirement exactly as written.

Simplify for non-native readers

Rewrite this section in plain English. Shorter sentences, no idioms, no jargon. Keep the meaning identical.

Tidy up typed notes

These are my typed notes from a meeting. Organise them into decisions, open questions and actions with an owner for each.

Ask in plain English

In three sentences, which [sites] and [categories] stand out in this table, and why?

Add a calculated column

Add a calculated column showing [days open], then explain the formula you wrote so I understand it.

Chart the pattern

Add a chart that makes the biggest trend obvious to a reader outside IT. Label the axes plainly.

Clean the data first

Format this range as a table with headers, flag duplicate rows and highlight rows with missing values.

Deck from your own document

Create an eight-slide briefing deck from [file name], using the template I have open. Audience: [OpCo IT leaders].

Set the slide rules

For each slide: a title of seven words or fewer, three or four short bullets, and a speaker note that expands the point in a calm, direct voice.

Refine one slide

Tighten slide four to five words per bullet, and turn slide seven into a two-column comparison.

Reorder the story

Reorganise this deck so the recommendation comes first and the supporting detail follows.

Summarise a long thread

Summarise this thread. Separate what is decided from what is still open, and list who owns each open item.

Find what is yours

List the actions assigned to me in this thread, with the date each was requested and what is still outstanding.

Three ways to reply

Draft three replies: A. confirm and commit to a date. B. agree in principle but ask two clarifying questions. C. redirect to the right owner with a warm handover.

Catch up after time away

Summarise what I missed in my inbox since [Monday], grouped by topic, three bullets each.

Your second message matters as much as the first. Click one to copy it.

Build your own prompt

Good prompts have four parts. Fill in the boxes, then copy.

Your prompt 

      
    

  






  
Before you press Enter

  

    
1. Is it OK to share?
Is everything I'm pasting or uploading allowed under Dover's data policy?

    
2. Is it a draft, not a decision?
Am I asking for help drafting, summarising or analysing, not for a decision about a person?

    
3. Will I check it?
Will I read and fix the answer before anyone else sees it?

  






  
If something doesn't work

  
I don't see the shield, or it looks like a personal account.
Sign out and sign back in with your Dover work account. The shield means enterprise data protection is on: your prompts and answers stay inside Dover's Microsoft 365 boundary and are not used to train AI models.
Copilot says it can't see my email, meetings or files.
That is expected in Copilot Chat. Without a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence it works with the web and the files you upload. With a licence, switch to Work mode.
I see two options above the chat box: Chat and Cowork.
Choose Chat for everything in this session. Cowork is a separate capability that is not part of today's demos.
I can't upload my file.
Try opening it from OneDrive instead, or paste the text in. If it is still blocked, the file's sensitivity label or a Dover policy may be stopping it. That is the control working as intended.
I don't see Schedule, Workspaces or Agent Mode.
Some features are switched on in stages. Ask your OpCo IT lead or Champion whether they are enabled for your account.
The answer looks wrong, or quotes something that isn't there.
Ask: “Show me where in the document you found that.” Check every number before you share anything. Copilot drafts; you decide.
How do I keep a prompt that worked?
Point at your prompt in Copilot Chat and choose Save prompt. Saved prompts appear in the Prompt gallery, where you can reuse them and share them with your team.
